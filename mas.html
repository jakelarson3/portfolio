Overview

Starting from a product brief, I designed, developed, and prototyped a sports bra in five weeks, working with the design, development, and sample room teams at MAS Bodyline. I served as designer, developer, and product manager throughout, choosing innovative materials and construction methods such as bonding and ultrasonic welding to support the function of the bra. Live fit sessions drove updates across two sample rounds. The internship showed me how product moves through a factory from brief to sample, and gave me firsthand respect for the expertise and craftsmanship manufacturers bring to that process.

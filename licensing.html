Overview

On a visit to Goldwin's Tokyo headquarters, I learned how The North Face works with Goldwin in Japan: Goldwin designs and produces product for the Japanese consumer, making the brand both high-quality and aspirational there. The challenge for this project was to apply that licensing model to another brand entering or growing in a new market. I chose Brooks Running, which recently launched a lifestyle footwear line, and Japan, one of the world's leading fashion markets, where success would give that line credibility. My plan licenses Brooks' lifestyle product to Beams, a Japanese retailer and brand known for collaborations with Arc'teryx, New Balance, and Hoka. That lets Beams create localized, elevated product that strengthens Brooks' style credibility beyond running. The project built my skills in merchandising, go-to-market planning, and persuasive presentation.

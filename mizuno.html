Overview

For my team's first tech pack, we took apart a finished Mizuno Synchro SL 2 and reverse engineered it into an industry-standard tech pack, identifying and documenting 44 components. As team lead and editor, I built and colored the CADs in Adobe Illustrator and created the component callouts and bill of materials. The project taught me how a tech pack is structured and what each section communicates to a factory, and it showed me how much complexity goes into building a single shoe.
